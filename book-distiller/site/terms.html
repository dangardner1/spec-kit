The most important part: nothing you type is saved on our servers. Book Distiller has no user accounts and no database. Your queries are processed in memory to generate a summary and are never written to storage.

1. What Book Distiller is

Book Distiller is a free tool that generates AI-powered distillations of books and topics. When you enter a title, it is sent to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) which produces an original summary — a thesis, framework, takeaways, and action plan. If you use the Listen feature, the summary text may also be sent to a text-to-speech provider (OpenAI) to generate narration.

Summaries are AI-generated interpretations, not excerpts or reproductions of the underlying books. They may contain errors, omissions, or characterizations the author would dispute. They are provided for general information and education only, and are not a substitute for reading the book — or for professional, medical, legal, or financial advice.

Affiliate links. Each distillation includes a link to buy the full book. These are affiliate links: if you purchase through one, Book Distiller may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects which book is shown, and no information about you is shared to make the link work — see the Privacy page.

2. Your data is not stored

To be explicit about how the service handles what you type:

  • No accounts, no database. There is nothing to sign up for and nowhere your queries are permanently recorded.
  • Transient processing only. Your query is forwarded to the AI provider solely to generate your summary, and results are held in short-lived server memory (up to 24 hours) so identical requests are faster. This cache is never written to disk and disappears automatically.
  • Your history stays in your browser. The "Recent" list on the home page is stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your device. We cannot see it, and you can erase it anytime by clearing your browser data.

For full details, see the Privacy page.
