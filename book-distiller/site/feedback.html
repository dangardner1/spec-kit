What helps most in a bug report

01

The book you typed

The exact title or topic you entered, so the issue can be reproduced.

02

What happened

What you saw versus what you expected — an error message, a blank screen, a wrong summary.

03

Your setup

Browser and device (e.g. Chrome on Windows, Safari on iPhone). It matters more than you'd think.
