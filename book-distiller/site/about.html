The vital 20% of any book, in the time it takes to read this page.

Book Distiller is a small AI tool: type a book title (or just a topic), and Claude reads it down to what actually matters — the core framework as an infographic, seven high-impact takeaways with real examples, and a practical plan to put it to work. No skimming, no book report, no 300 pages.

It applies the Pareto principle to itself: instead of a wall of text, every distillation follows the same repeatable shape, so you always know where to look.
